GhosTTalk is a powerful, customizable Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) app designed to give a voice to those with speech impairments. 

Features:
- **Customizable Books & Pages**: Create structured communication boards tailored to your needs.
- **Auditory Cues**: Support for auditory scanning, perfect for users with visual or motor impairments.
- **Hardware Integration**: Use Bluetooth switches or volume keys to interact with the app.
- **Advanced Audio Routing**: Separate audio output for cues and speech.
- **Row-by-Row Scanning**: Efficient navigation through grid layouts.
- **Generative AI (Coming Soon)**: Integration with Gemini for dynamic communication.

Empower your communication with GhosTTalk.
